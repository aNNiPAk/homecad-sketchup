# M5.7 Hardware Core decisions

- The research in `HARDWARE_RESEARCH.md` informs the assembly structure only. No geometry, Dynamic Component formulas, SKU table, textures or material assets from `References/` enter the extension. The JSON catalog contains an original generic HomeCAD rule and is bundled in RBZ as data, without executable expressions.
- The first supported assembly is a straight wooden drawer within a full-width `drawer_front`. The caller supplies all mounting choices that cannot be inferred safely: purchased SKU, nominal slide length and per-side clearance. One pair per drawer is part of the declared family unit, not a recommendation for a particular manufacturer's product.
- Cabinet local X/Y/Z is the sole placement frame. Drawer dimensions are stored in Cabinet parameters and revalidated on every update. Generated panel `part_key`s are stable; the Cabinet root retains the HomeCAD UUID. Child panel Groups are regenerated and are not separate domain mutation targets.
- Panel geometry uses the existing SketchUp `Entities#add_group`, `#add_face` and `Face#pushpull` path. The official [Entities API](https://ruby.sketchup.com/Sketchup/Entities.html) and [Face API](https://ruby.sketchup.com/Sketchup/Face.html) define these operations. The standalone fake checks planning and output shape; packaged smoke checks the real SketchUp kernel.
- `generate_cutlist` derives five panels and one selected slide pair from canonical parameters. The pre-existing manual `manufacturing.hardware` list remains independent and may intentionally add extra purchased items; HomeCAD does not silently deduplicate by SKU.
- M5.7.1 adds a cutlist warning for Cabinets with drawers because a caller-selected SKU and per-side clearance do not establish mounting or manufacturer compatibility. The warning is read-only and does not alter existing Cabinet parameters or revisions.
- The standalone geometry fake can verify each generated panel's local bounds and the stored assembly frame. A fresh SketchUp 26 process verifies the actual child Group bounds through `get_object`, matches them against `list_furniture_parts` and `generate_cutlist`, and exercises two drawers and Undo. These checks do not establish joinery, drilling or load ratings.
- This is not a manufacturer-specific fabrication recipe. The [Blum MERIVOBOX catalogue page](https://publications.blum.com/2026/catalogue/en/259/) specifies dimensional relationships for one variant, but those constants are not applied to this generic wooden drawer. Model-specific adapters need a complete technical source, a version, applicability limits and permission to distribute any geometry.
