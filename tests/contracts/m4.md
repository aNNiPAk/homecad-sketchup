# M4 Furniture Core contract

M4 introduces one generated Furniture domain type: `furniture.cabinet`. Cabinet parameters and relationships are the source of truth; primitive mutations may not edit the Cabinet or generated nested parts. All public lengths and coordinates are millimeters, and angles are degrees.

The Cabinet root uses the existing metadata fields `homecad_id`, `type`, `schema_version`, `revision`, and `generated`; its type is `furniture.cabinet`, `generated` is true, and creation starts at revision 1. `FurnitureData` stores canonical parameter and relationship JSON. Nested generated parts use `type=furniture.part`, `part_key`, and `part_kind`; they do not receive independent HomeCAD UUIDs.

## Cabinet frame and placement

Cabinet origin is back-left-bottom. Local X is width, Y is back-to-front, and Z is bottom-to-top. The right-handed case envelope is X `[0,width_mm]`, Y `[0,depth_mm]`, Z `[0,height_mm]`. Dimensions are represented by generated geometry; the root transform may contain rotation and translation only.

World placement is `{ "mode":"world", "origin_mm":[x,y,z], "rotation_degrees":n }`, with rotation about global Z. Wall placement is `{ "mode":"wall", "wall_id":"uuid", "offset_mm":n, "bottom_mm":n, "side":"positive_v|negative_v", "clearance_mm":n }`. The Cabinet occupies Wall U `[offset_mm, offset_mm + width_mm]`.

For `positive_v`, Cabinet X=Wall U, Y=Wall V, Z=global +Z. Its origin is at Wall-local `(offset_mm, thickness/2 + clearance_mm, bottom_mm)`. For `negative_v`, X=-Wall U, Y=-Wall V, Z=global +Z; origin is at `(offset_mm + width_mm, -thickness/2 - clearance_mm, bottom_mm)`. Both frames satisfy X × Y = Z. Wall-local parameters remain the source of truth when a Wall rotates, moves, or reverses direction.

## Parameters and generated parts

`create_cabinet` requires width, depth, and height. Panel thickness defaults to 18 mm, back thickness to 4 mm, shelves/fronts to empty arrays, detail level to `construction`, placement to world origin, and name to `Cabinet`. Back thickness may be zero; zero means the Cabinet has no back panel. Detail levels are `concept` and `construction`.

Construction is a frameless case: full-height left/right sides; top and bottom between sides; an optional back between sides and top/bottom at Y=0; shelf solids between sides from Y=back thickness through Y=depth. With no back, shelves start at Y=0 and span the full Cabinet depth. `shelf_z_mm[i]` is shelf bottom elevation and shelf thickness equals panel thickness. At most 32 shelves are allowed.

Front records have unique `key`, `kind` (`door`, `drawer_front`, or `fixed_panel`), `x_mm`, `z_mm`, positive width/height/thickness, and `hinge` (`left`, `right`, or `none`). Fronts fit within Cabinet X/Z and may not overlap. Construction fronts start at Y=depth and extend in +Y by their thickness. Concept detail creates a five-surface case envelope open at the front; configured fronts are plane faces at Y=depth, without a coincident envelope face behind them. Hardware, drawer boxes, and rails are not modeled.

Construction parts are nested generated Groups with `type=furniture.part`, `part_key`, and `part_kind`, but no public HomeCAD UUID. Logical keys are `left_side`, `right_side`, `bottom`, `top`, `back`, `shelf:<index>`, and `front:<key>`. The part schedule is calculated from parameters and remains independent of child entity IDs and detail level.

## Tools and mutations

Public tools: `get_furniture_frame`, `list_furniture_parts`, `create_cabinet`, `update_furniture_object`, and `delete_furniture_object`. Updates preserve root entity and UUID. Each successful mutation is one SketchUp Undo operation; failed validation starts no operation and changes no geometry, parameters, relationships, transform, or revision.

`FurnitureData` owns canonical parameter/relationship JSON. `WallAttachment` is a generic derived host projection with `wall_id`, `offset_mm`, `bottom_mm`, `side`, `clearance_mm`, `span_u_mm`, and `span_z_mm`. Cabinet storage derives the spans from `width_mm` and `height_mm`; the projection helper has no Furniture dependency. A world placement removes only HomeCAD-owned attachment attributes. Wall updates validate both occupied U and Z spans before mutation, relocate dependent roots without rebuilding internal geometry, and increment dependent revisions only if effective transforms change. Wall deletion rejects attached Furniture unless `cascade=true`, then deletes architecture dependencies, Room dependencies, and attached Furniture in one Undo operation after serializing tombstones.

Direct Furniture updates compare canonical editable parameters (`name`, dimensions, panel/back thickness, shelves, fronts, detail level, and placement). Any semantic state change increments the Cabinet revision exactly once, even when the effective world transform is unchanged (for example Wall A to Wall B or Wall to world detach). A normalized no-op starts no SketchUp operation and does not rebuild geometry. Host-induced Wall relocation leaves Furniture parameters unchanged and increments Cabinet revision only when its effective root transform changes. Placement-only updates do not regenerate internal parts.

Every method requires `furniture.core.v1`; protocol version remains 1. Inspection tools are read-only. Create is non-idempotent; update/delete are destructive and non-idempotent. MCP annotations are hints, not enforcement.

Each create, update, or delete that changes model state is one `HomeCAD::Operation` and returns the shared mutation envelope. Input/fit validation is completed before the operation starts; generator failures abort the operation. Wall updates preflight generic U/Z attachment spans against the proposed Wall before any Wall or Furniture geometry changes. Effective host relocation increments the Cabinet revision once; a Wall update that leaves the Cabinet transform unchanged does not.
