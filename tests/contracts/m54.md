# M5.4 manufacturing cutlist contract

`manufacturing.cutlist.v1` adds read-only `generate_cutlist(target,limit=50,offset=0)`.
The target resolves uniquely to a root Cabinet or KitchenRun. `limit` is 1..100; results
include `records`, `total`, `offset`, `has_more`, `warnings`, and `units=mm`.

Cabinet `manufacturing.parts` is keyed by logical `part_key` and may specify
`material_id`, `grain_axis` (`length`, `width`, `none`, or null), `sku`, and
`edge_band` with `length_start`, `length_end`, `width_start`, `width_end`.
Null means unknown or unspecified. `manufacturing.hardware` contains explicit unique
`key`, `sku`, and integer `quantity` (1..10000); there is no inferred hardware.
The maximum is 128 hardware records. Unknown part keys and invalid values are rejected
before mutation. Kitchen modules may carry the same manufacturing fields and material IDs.

In the existing Furniture part schedule, `width_mm` is the first cut-plane axis and
`height_mm` is the second cut-plane axis. The new `length_mm` equals that second axis;
`width_mm` retains its prior meaning, so no legacy consumer changes dimension semantics.
Edge `length_start/end` refers to the two ends along the second axis and
`width_start/end` to the two ends along the first. The axes are never sorted by size.
Each panel record has stable `part_key`, `source_object_id`, quantity, dimensions, material,
grain, four edges and optional SKU. Hardware has no cut dimensions.

KitchenRun yields generic carcass records for non-appliance modules. Oven, hob,
dishwasher and fridge are concept appliances and produce warnings instead of invented
parts. Sink and drawer modules warn that internal mechanisms are not modeled. Results
are derived from run/module parameters and stable semantic keys, not SketchUp child IDs,
and are stable across Cabinet concept/construction LOD. No prices, Redcut integration,
CSV export, or geometry-based measurement is claimed.
